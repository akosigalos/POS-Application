# Demonstration and Examination Plan

## Preparation

1. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.
2. Begin with a clean order.
3. Use a desktop or tablet-width display first, then demonstrate a narrow mobile width.

## Demonstration Flow

1. Select products and show their names and prices.
2. Increase and decrease quantities, then remove an item.
3. Use category filters and product search; show the empty search state.
4. Continue to Order Summary and verify item subtotals.
5. Select each discount option and explain subtotal, discount, VAT, and final total.
6. Demonstrate insufficient cash validation, then complete a valid cash payment and explain the change.
7. Complete QR and card payment simulations.
8. Open a receipt and verify its transaction number, pricing details, payment method, amount paid, change, and paid status.
9. Use Print Receipt and Download Receipt; explain that downloads are `.txt` files.
10. Open Order History and explain the Daily Sales Report.
11. Submit customer feedback, then show Feedback History and its rating summary.
12. Start a New Transaction and confirm that the active order and receipt state reset.

## Evidence Checklist

- Save real application screenshots in `docs/evidence/` using the required filenames.
- Capture both desktop and mobile examples.
- Include visible values that demonstrate totals, VAT, payment result, and receipt information.
